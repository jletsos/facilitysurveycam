Privacy Policy for FacilitySurveyCam

Last updated: June 7, 2026

Overview

FacilitySurveyCam is owned and operated by Jordan Letsos. This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

Data We Collect

  • Camera — to capture inspection photos
  • Photo Library — to save captured photos to your FacilitySurveyCam album
  • Location (GPS) — to embed precise coordinates into photo EXIF data at the moment of capture

How Your Data Is Used

All facility metadata is entered by you and embedded locally into photo files using EXIF standards. GPS coordinates are written into the photo file at capture time on your device. No data is transmitted to any external server, database, or third party. The App has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no cloud sync of its own.

Data Storage

All photos and embedded metadata are stored exclusively in your device's Photos library under a dedicated FacilitySurveyCam album. The App does not maintain any separate database or remote storage.

Data Sharing

The App does not share your data with anyone. When you choose to share a photo via AirDrop, email, or messaging, the embedded metadata travels with that photo as part of the standard JPEG file format. You are responsible for ensuring shared photos comply with your organisation's data handling policies.

iCloud and Backups

If you have iCloud Photos enabled, photos saved by the App may be automatically backed up to iCloud under Apple's terms of service. This is controlled entirely by your iOS settings and is not managed by the App.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks, or crash reporting tools.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated date.
