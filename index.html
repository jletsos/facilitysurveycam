Features

Everything in the Image File

Metadata travels with the photo — no app, no database, no extra step required.

📁

Structured Filenames

Every photo is automatically named by site, building, floor, room, asset class, and context. No more IMG_0287.

📍

GPS Coordinates

Precise location is embedded in EXIF at the moment of capture — visible in iOS Photos, Google Photos, and any EXIF viewer.

🏷️

EXIF Metadata

Site ID, building, floor, room, asset class, and photo context are written directly into the image file using industry-standard EXIF.

🗂️

Dedicated Album

All inspection photos are saved to a FacilitySurveyCam album. Browse, rename, and delete from within the app.

🌙

Dark Mode

Full dark mode support. Automatically matches your iOS system setting.

🔒

Private by Design

No accounts. No servers. No analytics. All data stays on your device and in your Photos library.

How It Works

1

Fill in the Hierarchy

3

Done
